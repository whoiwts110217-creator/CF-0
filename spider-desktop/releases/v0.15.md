## Concrete repaired failure — two before/after regressions

**Original v0.14 bug:** In `chrome_extension/background.js`, after `await chrome.debugger.sendCommand('Input.insertText', ...)`, Spider immediately dispatched `Input.dispatchKeyEvent` with Enter, without rechecking STOP, the selected conversation or the desktop owner gate. If STOP arrived while the asynchronous insert was unresolved, the queued Enter could still send after STOP. Another STOP at the Python controller could likewise invalidate the gate while insertion was pending.

**Before repair:** Two new v0.14 Chrome regression scenarios both **FAIL** (15/17 passed, 2/17 failed; Enter did dispatch after STOP/revoked gate).

**Fix:** After CDP text insertion resolves, v0.15 explicitly checks: `interrupted`, `tabCheck` (same selected chat URL/active tab), a new authenticated desktop `POST /gate` for the same leased job, and `interrupted` again. Any refused step prevents initiating Enter and retains the `maybeSentId` uncertain-send checkpoint. No replays, implicit retries or extra paid calls.

**After repair:** All **17 Node tests PASS**. Added Python-server ↔ actual JS source-in-synthetic-worker scenario while waiting at the asynchronous insertion boundary: the **real** `Controller.stop()` revokes the leased job and token, then the JS worker resumes; **no Enter**, `maybeSentId` preserved, the outcome remains unknown and requires human review. This brings total clean ZIP source tests to **168 Python**, including the new end-to-end stop case. The service worker was NOT running as a real installed Chrome extension in this environment.

**Limits:** A CDP insert may already leave an **unsent draft** in the selected ChatGPT composer. STOP cannot retract an Enter command that was already dispatched or guarantee that the real ChatGPT site did not accept a send. Do not report definite cancellation; inspect the exact conversation and uncertain-send indicator before clearing it. This is risk reduction, not a proved atomic cancellation.
