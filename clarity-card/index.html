One hard question. One clear next step.

The Clarity Card is a small evidence-to-action worksheet. It helps you turn a vague problem into something you can actually test, without pretending a guess is proof.

Your writing stays in this browser page. This tool does not send, save, or track responses. Download a text file or print your work if you want to keep it. It is a planning aid, not professional legal, medical or financial advice.

Fill any fields you want. The card updates immediately.
