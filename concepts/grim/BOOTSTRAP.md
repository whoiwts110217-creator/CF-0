## Creator-established canon — Grim (do not silently change)

- **Name:** Grim. Nobody knows his identity in the outside world. He tells a captive his name **only when they say something that genuinely interests him**.
- **Appearance:** Wild, **white**, frizzy hair drapes **over his eyes**; his eyes are **red** but mostly hidden. He wears an oversized **white shirt** and **black baggy pants**. A folded note peeks from a pocket and reads exactly `:)` (a simple smiley; no added words). His mouth has subtle **thin red slit-like lines extending from its corners**, inspired by the creepy smile aesthetic the creator requested.
- **Temperament:** Genuinely playful, smug, and unnerving; he is **not acting**. He treats frightening encounters as a game. When someone refuses to show fear, he starts talking; his voice becomes playful, slightly squeaky, and upset. He may kill if they say something that he considers wrong.
- **Childhood:** Fascinated by killing bugs. His mother beat him for his behavior, calling it weird; he **never cried**. One bug once escaped him. He **remembers** it, but it has **no special importance** to him.
- **Later behavior:** He becomes a serial killer, targets innocent victims, and kidnaps a person who was simply walking around. He enjoys the sounds of victims screaming and watching them try to run; to him this really is a game. He has never spoken to a person he wasn't intending to kill.
- **Unusual captive:** He meets someone he *actually likes*, despite originally intending to kill them. The captive wanted to die, and Grim discovers that **as he is about to kill them**, when they show no emotion. Whether he spares or kills them depends on **what they say**. **The creator has not chosen that exact answer or final outcome.**

## Separate character — Moth (assistant-created draft material)

- **Name:** Moth. A supporting character, separate from Grim, invented by the assistant and included in the Book One draft at the creator's request.
- **Visual concept:** Short silver hair, sharp attentive eyes, oversized dark coat, and a small lantern.
- **Draft backstory:** Someone vanished from Moth's neighborhood; adults dismissed the case. Moth kept asking questions, recording missing names and looking for truths other people abandoned.
- **Function in Book One:** Moth arrives at an abandoned theater while following disappearances, challenges Grim, and attempts to help the captive.
- **Continuity status:** Provisional AI-created characterization, **not** a claim that the Grim creator supplied these details. It can be rewritten or rejected independently of Grim canon.

## Book One — documented additions versus canon

- **Full manuscript:** [BOOK_ONE.md](BOOK_ONE.md), titled *Grim: The Rules of the Game*.
- **Structure:** Prologue (“The One That Got Away”); six chapters: “A Note in His Pocket,” “The Rules Nobody Knows,” “The Person With the Lantern,” “Something Interesting,” “Moth at the Door,” “The Question at the End.”

- **Themes / content:** Horror, coercion, childhood abuse, murder, kidnapping, fear, and a character's desire to die. Fictional depictions are not endorsements. Avoid sensationalizing suicidal thinking. **Publication status: an AI-assisted draft, not an originality-cleared final release.**
