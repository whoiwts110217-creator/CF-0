What the $15 pilot includes

How a real pilot request works

  1. Open the public-safe inquiry form and describe only a non-sensitive, publicly discussable problem.
  2. The project owner reviews feasibility, public sources, scope and delivery capacity. It may decline or narrow the request.
  3. If accepted, the exact price, private payment and delivery mechanism, deadline, and cancellation/refund process are confirmed before you pay. The site's current Cash App donation link is not a service checkout or order confirmation.
  4. Only after payment and scope are confirmed is a one-page brief prepared and delivered through the agreed route. No order or sale is considered complete without a verified receipt and delivered artifact.

If the project accepts an order but cannot deliver the agreed brief, it will offer a refund of the service fee using the agreed payment mechanism. Exact refund handling, third-party fees and any applicable buyer protections must be confirmed with the buyer before collecting payment.

Why this exists

This pilot tests whether people value a short, honest, usable decision aid enough to pay for a tailored version. The free Clarity Card remains available without subscribing, buying, or even opening a GitHub account. There are no customer testimonials, order totals, or measured sales outcomes to report yet.
