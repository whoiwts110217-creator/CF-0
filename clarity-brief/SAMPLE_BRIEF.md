# SAMPLE — one-page Clarity Brief (not a completed client assignment)

**Project:** Caveman Focus public visitor comprehension  
**Question:** Can a first-time visitor explain what Caveman Focus does after one minute on the homepage?  
**Owner:** Demonstration only; no participants were recruited or observed.

## What we know (with boundaries)

- The public homepage presents the framework and links to a working free Clarity Card. Source: https://whoiwts110217-creator.github.io/CF-0/
- Existing automated and browser checks can confirm that the pages render and specific controls work. They do **not** show what actual visitors learned or would pay.
- **Uncollected:** visitor comprehension, use, satisfaction, and conversion data.

## One falsifiable hypothesis

After reading the home page for 60 seconds, at least **2 of 3 consenting first-time adult volunteers** can explain the project's purpose without prompting, with the answer reasonably matching the prespecified description: "a method and tools for simplifying questions, testing ideas, and keeping evidence distinct from claims."

## Smallest useful test

1. Record the exact page version/date and the fixed definition above.
2. Recruit three willing adults unfamiliar with the project; no names or identifying information in the public report.
3. Show each person the homepage for 60 seconds.
4. Ask one neutral prompt: "What is this project for?"
5. Record responses with permission, then independently classify each as aligned / partly aligned / not aligned, preserving disagreement.
6. Publish only anonymized aggregate counts and page version; do **not** infer impact or market demand from three responses.

## Decision / evidence gate

- If fewer than 2 align: revise homepage first-screen copy, preserve the original and rerun the same test.
- If 2 or 3 align: the **preliminary comprehension threshold** passes for this tiny sample; repeat with a broader group before claiming accessibility or demand.
- If responses disagree on meaning: investigate the specific ambiguity instead of selectively scoring.
- If no test runs: **STATUS = UNTESTED**, not success or failure.

## Deliverable scope

This example demonstrates the kind of evidence, prediction and practical next action a paid brief can contain. It is not a testimonial, scientific proof, buyer result, grant award or verified revenue.
