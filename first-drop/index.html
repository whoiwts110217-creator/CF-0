What is Caveman Focus?

Caveman Focus explores a basic method: simplify complexity without losing essentials, then move from guess → hypothesis → evidence → proof. It is being applied to public documentation, AI-assisted automation, creative work and experiments. Some source and tests are public; the complete system is still under development, not a finished product.

The founding Hall of Fame

You can support Caveman Focus in any amount and opt into public recognition as a founding supporter. If you wish to be listed, write HALL OF FAME — DISPLAY: [your chosen public name or handle] in the Cash App payment note. Anonymous contributions are welcome too.

Names are listed only after a payment is verified and the chosen display name is confirmed as consensual. A payment alone does not authorize publishing personal information. There are no verified public supporter names posted on this page.

Recognition is a thank-you, not an ownership share, investment, purchase, token, award, or guaranteed benefit. Contributions are voluntary and not tax-deductible charitable donations. Nothing here promises a financial return.

Two different kinds of credit: this founding supporter wall thanks verified voluntary supporters who choose to be named. The separate contributor Hall of Fame records reviewed technical contributions. Donating does not buy technical credit or rights to the project.
