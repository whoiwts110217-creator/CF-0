**Public research notebook | Published November 22, 2026 | Status: all 2,000 entries unverified**

> **Important: These are guesses, not theorems or proofs.** None of the 2,000 entries has been independently verified, formally proved, peer-reviewed, or established as a new mathematical discovery. The stated “RH lever” fields and “First check” fields are proposals for further investigation, not confirmations. Do not cite these as established mathematical results.

This public archive contains exactly 2,000 numbered speculative ideas, organized into 92 topic families. Most entries assume the Riemann Hypothesis (RH) is true. That assumption is a premise for exploration, not a claim that RH has been proved. Any discussion of an announced RH proof in previous versions of the materials is not an endorsement or verification of that claim.

## Contents

- `guesses.json` — all 2,000 original research entries, preserved from the supplied archive without edits to entries; each includes an ID, family, kind, guess, RH lever, and initial check.
- `index.html` — searchable public viewer, with its introduction updated to emphasize the unverified status of every entry.

## How to view

Download this folder and serve it locally as a simple static site so `index.html` can fetch `guesses.json`. From this folder, run `python -m http.server 8000` and visit `http://localhost:8000` in a browser. The data file is readable directly without the viewer.
