## Audit findings — serious limitations, not cosmetic defects

| Finding | Evidence in v0.1 | Consequence | v0.2 candidate response |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A1. Mixed response grammar — material** | `B-EXEC`, `B-FUND`, `B-PROOF`, `B-LOG`, `I-FUND`, `I-PROOF`, `D-EXEC`, `D-FUND`, `D-PROOF`, `D-LOG` are phrased as questions while the answer format is TRUE/FALSE | Respondents may interpret the task as YES/NO, potentially affecting measurement rather than conceptual knowledge | All twelve candidate items are declarative claims to be judged TRUE or FALSE; no question-mark stems |
| **A2. Closely parallel or near-repeat wording — material** | `B-EXEC` vs `D-EXEC`: public message without local run proof; `B-FUND` vs `D-FUND`: awarded funds vs received cash; `B-LOG` vs `D-LOG`: erased failures; `I-EXEC` parallels the in-lesson transfer example | A delayed answer can reflect recognizing a repeated formula or subject familiarity rather than applying a learned general rule | Rotate contexts (backup/site, media upload/report; crowdfund, freelance contract/shop; game patch, game rollback/design archive). The four underlying concepts remain fixed, so **independence of difficulty is still unproven** |
| **A3. Obvious qualifying cues — material** | `B-EXEC` says “By itself”; `I-FUND` asks “already”; `I-PROOF` says “alone”; `I-LOG` is an explicitly helpful-sounding claim | A test-wise respondent may guess from style and qualifiers instead of understanding evidence | Rewrite more uniformly as scenario + declarative conclusion. Some necessary logical qualifiers and positivity/negativity cues remain; a blinded expert should still revise |
| **A4. Predictable answer-key pattern — moderate** | Baseline and delayed keys in identical published order are `F T T F`; immediate `T F F T`, and worksheet publicly reveals the 2/2 balance | People can exploit publicly visible structure and cross-wave pattern; announcing fixed balance further undermines interpretability | Candidate order and key patterns differ by wave (`T F F T`, `F T T F`, `T F T F`). This is **not randomization**; the public key still leaks, and balance should not be a participant instruction |
| **A5. Guessing, ceiling and poor scale resolution — critical** | Each wave has only four binary items; the sheet expects 0–4 | Under independent fair guessing, the mean is 2 correct of 4, and 5/16 of guesses achieve at least 3 correct. A small raw score difference is noisy. A 48-hour sample can also be biased by who returns | Candidate retains binary form to keep design comparable, but is explicitly **unvalidated**. A serious pilot should consider more items or justified constructed-response scoring after expert review |
| **A6. Unequal forms and outcome interpretation — critical** | Each wave uses different contexts and has no empirical item-difficulty calibration or blinded validity review | Score increases/drops can reflect form difficulty, not memory. Equal topic count and answer balance do not establish parallel measurement forms | Candidate adds versioned source traces and demands expert item review/calibration before treating baseline, immediate and delayed scores as directly comparable |
| **A7. Public answer-key contamination — critical** | The development key is in the same public repository as the worksheet | Learners can see or memorize answers; a public worksheet is unsuitable for credible blinded trial outcomes | Preserve the public key **as development-only**, with explicit warning. Before recruitment, author independently reviewed locked private materials and separate key custody; do not pretend a public GitHub key is secure |
| **A8. No authorized study execution — hard stop** | No adult informed consent, allocation, response storage, scheduled delayed sessions, follow-up eligibility or attrition evidence | Neither version is an observational dataset nor proof of efficacy | No analytics, uploads, timers, participant form or live allocation added. Proposed study stays blocked until separately authorized |

## Specific content validity notes

- **Execution evidence:** distinguish a remote receipt, a named host's scheduler evidence, and a wrapper outcome; do not claim a match alone *guarantees* physical execution or authenticates the worker.
- **Funding:** the claim under test is verified receipt of funds, not full accounting recognition of revenue under financial-reporting standards. Wording says “settled cash” or “bank received,” avoiding a blanket accounting pronouncement.
- **Universal proof:** a valid counterexample contradicts a universal proposition, whereas finite confirming examples are not themselves a proof of an unbounded one. Neither claim is new or an RH result.
- **Continuity:** preserve failure, repair, exact version and rerun separately; a passing later build should not rewrite earlier provenance.

## Evidence standards and remaining gaps

The draft improvements follow commonly recommended item-writing principles: declarative, single-concept true/false propositions; minimize cue words and negatives; avoid patterned keys; compare form difficulties instead of assuming parallel forms. References: [University of Waterloo, true/false guidance](https://uwaterloo.ca/centre-for-teaching-excellence/catalogs/tip-sheets/exam-questions-types-characteristics-and-suggestions); [University of Michigan CRLT](https://crlt.umich.edu/P8_0); [ASCD, Creating Student-Friendly Tests](https://ascd.org/el/articles/creating-student-friendly-tests); and [parallel-form measurement primer](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6759012/). These sources support the critique; they **do not validate CF-0's new items**.

**Internal conclusion:** v0.1 is suitable as a learning-design sketch but should *not* be used as a credible study instrument. v0.2 is a more consistent **candidate only**. Both remain publicly answer-keyed and uncalibrated. We have not done an independent blinded expert review, cognitive interviews, item discrimination analysis, response collection, ethics approval, randomized trial, delayed 48-hour test, or causal analysis. Claiming any of these happened would be false.

**Acceptance gate for future work:** First request independent subject-matter review for each item (clear answer, exactly one construct, no unfair cues, difficulty comparison, language accessibility). Revise/retest both forms and lock private answers. Only after owner authorization and participant consent should any enrollment, randomized Plain/Playful assignment, data collection or real delayed session occur. Report outcomes by arm with denominators, missingness and uncertainty, rather than percent-correct alone.

**Privacy, authority and rollback:** this audit and v0.2 candidates add no browser telemetry, personal-data forms, external messages, account access, funding actions, Mission Control changes, HP/AUTO-08 permissions, scheduled automations or CF-200 registry credits. To roll back, archive the v0.2 candidate and its tests while keeping v0.1 untouched; preserve this audit and all Git commits for provenance.
