## Research integrity (development only)

This candidate uses four short binary items per wave, covering `receipt`, `funding`, `hypothesis` and `continuity`. The statements are intentionally ordered differently across waves, but **order variation cannot equate difficulty** or prevent deliberate memorization of a public key.

**Scoring rule to consider for a future independently approved pilot:** one point per correct response, with a possible 0–4 on a *complete* wave; missing items are recorded missing, not automatically wrong. Separate completion, follow-up timing and attrition from outcomes. Do not silently compare 3 answered items against 4 answered items as equal totals. All scored outcomes would require consent and an actual independent delayed session. The public worksheet stores or submits nothing.

**Major open limitations:** 50% guessing on individual TRUE/FALSE items, very short scale, alternative-form difficulty unmeasured, potential ceiling effects, content-key leakage and word-cue learning. A formal study needs expert validity review, independent random assignment to matched teaching modes, private version-locked answer keys, ethically approved minimal-response handling and delayed-return logistics. These are not satisfied by publishing this document.
