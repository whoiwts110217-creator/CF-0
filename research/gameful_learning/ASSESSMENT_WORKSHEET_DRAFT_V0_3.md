**Status:** **PUBLIC DEVELOPMENT CANDIDATE — NOT AN ACTIVE RESEARCH STUDY.** These prompts and the companion [public development rubric](ASSESSMENT_RUBRIC_DRAFT_V0_3.json) are not secret. No participation, consent, randomization, response collection, external posting, timed reminder, login or data transmission is implemented.

**Common prompt for all twelve scenarios:** For the situation described, write **(1)** the strongest conclusion supported by the evidence and **(2)** one concrete reason or missing/present piece of evidence that justifies that conclusion. Aim for one sentence per part. Do not supply identifying or personal information. There is no TRUE/FALSE guessing choice.

## Development scoring plan (not for unapproved collection)

The development [rubric](ASSESSMENT_RUBRIC_DRAFT_V0_3.json) has two distinct one-point criteria per scenario: **one point for the warranted conclusion** and **one point for a concrete, valid reason or evidence boundary**. A complete four-item wave is 0–8 points, *not* the v0.1/v0.2 0–4 binary scale. A response may correctly recognize a conclusion without justifying it (one point), or identify the correct evidence boundary without expressing the conclusion (one point). A contrary claim cannot earn a conclusion point. Unanswered items remain **missing** pending a prespecified analysis rule; they are not silently zeros.

No computer tool or AI should automatically grade open text from this worksheet as valid research evidence. Before a consented study, develop a locked rubric with **two independent trained human raters**, masked to teaching condition and assessment wave wherever practical, use separate scoring, document agreement and adjudication, and conduct independent item review and cognitive interviews. A two-facet scale is a better prototype for showing reasoning than forced binary choices, but **not proven reliable or equivalent across three forms**. The 12 keyed anchors are public and might contaminate a real evaluation.

**Important design change:** Because this response format changes the score scale and the task, it is an **alternative instrument**, not a retroactive revision to a preregistered outcome. No original preregistration has been activated; before enrollment, the owner/research team would need to explicitly decide and lock which outcome is primary. The existing Plain/Playful demo remains self-selected and collects no research data.

**Rollback and provenance:** preserve this worksheet, [rubric](ASSESSMENT_RUBRIC_DRAFT_V0_3.json), [review/validation gate](ASSESSMENT_SCORING_REVIEW_V0_3.md), earlier versions, and revision history. Any failure must be documented rather than silently deleted.
