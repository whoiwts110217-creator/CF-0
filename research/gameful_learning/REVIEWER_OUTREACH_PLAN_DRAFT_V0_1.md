## What actually needs independent review

The [12-item review packet](INDEPENDENT_REVIEW_PACKET_V0_3.md) and [v0.3 worksheet](ASSESSMENT_WORKSHEET_DRAFT_V0_3.md) are ready for *external criticism*, not certification. Requested deliverable: review 12 item/rubric pairs for factual soundness, construct validity, separable 0–2 conclusion/evidence scoring, alternate correct responses, fairness/accessibility and unequal difficulty across baseline/immediate/delayed. A **one-page preliminary triage** is useful if a full review is unavailable. Ask reviewers to disclose expertise, conflicts and fee before work; they may refuse or require payment.

Do **not** claim reviewing this public material creates a blinded test, equates score forms, verifies 48-hour learning, establishes participants' outcomes or implies institutional endorsement. All answer keys are public; a real randomized study would require new private study forms, consent and a locked protocol.
