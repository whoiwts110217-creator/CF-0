## The challenge

Can you improve a decision by separating what was **observed**, what was **assumed**, and what would **change your mind**?

**Example:** A project publishes a page and receives no confirmed supporters. Someone concludes, "The project has no value."

- **Observed:** no supporter contribution has been confirmed.
- **Assumed:** the page reached an appropriate audience; those who saw it understood it; a lack of payment proves lack of value.
- **Test:** identify one permission-based, relevant distribution channel; publish only with authorization; measure verified exposure or genuine responses independently of donations.
