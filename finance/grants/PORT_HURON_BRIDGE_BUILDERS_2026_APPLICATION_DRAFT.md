## Public-safe application narrative — draft, not submitted

**Project title:** Port Huron Neighbor-to-Neighbor Clarity Workshop

**What:** Offer two free, welcoming community conversations in Port Huron between March and August 2027. Each brings neighbors from different backgrounds and ages together to explore one practical local question, compare their assumptions, identify reliable evidence, and propose one achievable follow-up action. The method uses the already-working, free [Caveman Focus Clarity Card](https://whoiwts110217-creator.github.io/CF-0/clarity-card/) as a prompt, plus printed large-text cards for people who do not want to use a phone.

**Why community benefit rather than promoting a company:** Participants would work in pairs or small mixed groups; the output is a shared, voluntarily contributed *community idea board*, not a customer funnel. Sessions are open without payment, donation, email signup or a need to use AI. Participants are not recruited or guaranteed yet. Organizers would ask consent before publicly sharing any participant words or images. Local issue examples could include access to town events, intergenerational understanding, or finding resources—chosen with participating neighbors, not predetermined by the organizer.

**Where:** Port Huron, Michigan. An accessible public community venue is being sought; **no library space or city property has been reserved**. An inquiry was emailed to the St. Clair County Library System on November 9; the application must not name it as a committed partner. If a venue is not secured, do not claim the sessions can be held there.

**Who:** An independent individual project organizer is the *proposed* lead. Formal legal applicant name, reliable phone, mailing address, any co-organizers, and prior-grant history remain to be confirmed. Do not register as an organization or informal neighbor group unless that accurately describes real participants. No funded staff or sponsor has been secured. MML Community Microgrant guidelines allow individuals according to the FAQ, but the application screen requires clarification.

**When:** Proposed planning/contact/venue permission January–February 2027; sessions approximately April and July 2027, subject to venue/participants; complete report by August 31, 2027 (within required Jan 1–Sep 30 2027).

**Success criteria:** Hold two free sessions with consent, invite diverse residents using accessible public outreach, record anonymous attendance only, record at least one collaboratively drafted evidence-to-action proposal per session, and publish a de-identified reflection on which ideas participants found useful and what remained unproven. No fabricated attendance target or guaranteed participation. Meeting count and records are acceptance evidence, not projected outcomes.
