# Creative Work — Public Titles and Summaries

- **Mushroomhead / Blue Water County / Poho**: animated anthology, including `DIRT BAG` (Dre), `The Port of Dopes` and a Poho finale. Big Bertha recurs; stylized real location inspirations.
- **SYNAPSE**: Kyōji redemption story and brother Shōichi, Spiegel/Sterben avatars, layered reality and long-term consequences. Existing franchises are inspirational references, not owned content.
- **Single-Dot Universe Book I**: separately evolving novel with V7/V8 checkpoints and V9 narrative corrections.
- **BROKEN LOGIC**: original concept; full source incomplete.
- **“And then he mistook the sunset for God”**: Elias Vale opening and grandfather/radio sunset treatment, with latter direction favored later.
- **Philosopher's Stone** scene: carpenter, stone hammer, nature's gaze/horror.
- **Tactile book machine**, mathematical/AI concept art and further archive fragments.

Do not republish full unpublished scripts or personal passages without identifying and reviewing the specific source.
