# Caveman Focus — Foundation, Ideals and Ownership

**Attribution:** Kiara Lynn Rose Smith's Caveman Focus.

**Primary definition:** A framework for thinking, creating and researching by simplifying complexity without losing essentials, where clarity is the desired outcome.

**Stages:** Guess → Hypothesis → Evidence → Proof. In empirical work, replace formal proof with appropriately qualified independent verification when necessary.

### Philosophical themes
- Preserve old claims, contradictions, failures and original dates.
- An AI's convincing explanation is not complete access to its actual causal processes.
- Neither belief, spite, doubt nor curiosity alone suffices to discover a "trick"; observer, tools, circumstances and phenomenon must all be considered.
- **Philosopher's Stone:** a carpenter wields a stone hammer; a tree loses a limb and a rock loses a "child". Humans appear eldritch when **nature looks back in horror** at the hammer's use.
- Inherence of a stone/person is conceptually distinct from assigned purpose or metaphorical function; no literal stone sentience has been established.
- Apparent magic should inspire investigation, not automatic certainty.
- Attribution can coexist with ideas spreading; credit is not proof of patent originality or actual theft.

**Caveman Focus** is the parent method, not identical to RH, Single-Dot, the fictional narratives, the business, or the automation implementation.
