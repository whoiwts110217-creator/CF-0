## Owner-defined operating priorities

1. **Automation:** execution, transport, scheduling, receipts and recovery; currently eliminate the human PowerShell copy/paste loop.
2. **Priority intelligence:** make owner decisions govern task selection, overrides and accountability.
3. **Financial operations:** legitimate products, donations and grants; only verified collected payments count as income.
4. **Other projects concurrently:** research, creative work and experiments when resources permit.

Earlier cash-first efforts and earlier Sidecar prototypes are historical stages, not replacements for this priority order.
