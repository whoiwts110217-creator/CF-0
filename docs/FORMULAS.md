## Riemann Hypothesis (standard)
```text
Nontrivial ζ zero ρ: conjectured Re(ρ)=1/2.
ξ(s) = ½ s(s−1) π^(−s/2) Γ(s/2) ζ(s)
ξ(s) = ξ(1−s)
w_ρ = 1−1/ρ ; |w_ρ|=1 iff Re(ρ)=½ (ρ≠0)
ψ(x)=Σ_{n≤x}Λ(n)
RH ⇔ ψ(x)=x+O(√x log²x)
α=inf{a: ψ(x)−x=O(x^a)}
```
Under standard conditions, `α=½` is equivalent to RH. Finite evidence cannot certify the asymptotic result.

### Weil positivity
Let `h=f*tilde(f)` with suitable real compactly supported smooth `f`; on real frequencies `h_hat(t)=|f_hat(t)|²`. Complex extension needs correct analytic conjugation. Density, continuity and transfer are open obligations in the explored proof route.

### Gaussian quartet
`S₄=4 exp(a(ε²−γ²)) cos(2aεγ)` as a diagnostic in a historical branch, not a proof criterion.

## Proposed capability-frontier formalisms
```text
Reach(I,A): least closure of initial states I under permitted action set A.
F_struct = Reach(I,A)
F_info = reliably attainable policies given an observation model
F_budget = outcomes achievable with resource cost ≤ B
Δ_task = (newly solved − regressed) / tested
```
A macro that merely composes existing actions need not extend unlimited reachability, though it can expand **budget-limited** feasibility. Δ_task is not proof of a new irreducible action.
