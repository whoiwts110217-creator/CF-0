# Example $5 Clarity Check — Caveman Focus First Drop
**Demonstration only, November 9, 2026.** Not a paid client, external endorsement, result or measured conversion. A model exercise applied to our own public [First Drop landing page](https://whoiwts110217-creator.github.io/CF-0/first-drop/); the page was viewed in a public browser in this work session. A real buyer's critique will be specific to their supplied public URL.

## What a new visitor could understand
The page invites voluntary support for Caveman Focus, a public project exploring clarity and AI-assisted tools, with optional recognition for verified supporters.

## Three practical points of friction
1. **The headline is intriguing but underspecified.** "Get in before the world knows what it is" invites curiosity but does not immediately tell a first-time visitor which working result exists. A one-line value statement above the fold would establish concrete purpose.
2. **The first action requests support before demonstrating value.** The free Clarity Card link is present, but listed behind the Cash App button. A primary "Try the free tool" action may reduce skepticism among visitors who are not ready to donate. This is an untested recommendation.
3. **The project is transparent but too broad for a first glance.** It mentions documentation, automation, creative work and experiments. A screenshot or 2-sentence sample showing *one* concrete problem → hypothesis → next step would make the method easier to evaluate.

## Testable hypothesis (not proof)
If the page leads with the free tool plus a worked example before its funding invitation, more first-time visitors will understand the project and voluntarily explore support. **No visitors, conversions, or outcomes were measured.**

## Smallest next action
Make one reversible copy/layout variation moving "Use the free Clarity Card" before "Support on Cash App", and show a simple example. With consented user testing, ask three first-time viewers to explain the offer in their own words.

**Completion evidence:** Record whether at least two of three can identify the project's practical output without prompting, and separately whether they found the support path. This tiny exercise is not a representative conversion trial.

**Scope and limits:** This critique is about one public landing page only. It is not a security, legal, business, conversion-rate, or accessibility certification. It creates no guarantee of sales.
