## Copy for genuine feedback communities

**Headline:** I built a free Clarity Card for turning confusing questions into testable next steps — can you break it?

**Post:**
I've been building Caveman Focus around an intentionally simple workflow: guess → hypothesis → evidence → proof. The challenge is keeping the evidence attached when a problem gets complicated.

There's now a free, browser-local [Clarity Card](https://whoiwts110217-creator.github.io/CF-0/clarity-card/) you can try without an account. It helps you write down what you think, what could disprove it, and one concrete next action. You can export your own result.

I'm looking for specific criticism, not applause. Which field is unclear? Where would the exercise produce a misleading answer? What one change would make it useful in a real project?

The [source and status](https://github.com/whoiwts110217-creator/CF-0) are public. This is an experimental project, not a proven research breakthrough or mature product.
