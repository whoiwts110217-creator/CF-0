# 30 Speculative Automation Candidates

**Candidate ideas, not historical firsts or validated scientific breakthroughs.**

1. Missing-Verb Engine — discover an absent operation.
2. Anti-Task Machine — eliminate recurring work.
3. Failure Genome — cross-domain failure patterns.
4. Capability Embryos — test/promote tiny abilities.
5. Impossible-Goal Decomposer — identify missing facts or resources.
6. Automation Evolution Chamber — compare competing architectures.
7. Self-Inventing Measurement — propose new metrics/instruments.
8. Goal-Mutation Detector — recognize obsolete objectives.
9. Contradiction Reactor — design separating experiments.
10. Historical Mind Emulator — provenance-constrained reasoning reconstructions.
11. Unknown-Space Cartographer — map identifiable gaps.
12. Proof-Debt Collector — track unjustified claims.
13. Ontological Mutation Engine — propose useful new categories.
14. Forgotten-Invention Excavator — mine old projects for usable methods.
15. Counterfactual History Laboratory — compare possible histories carefully.
16. Scientific Instrument Inventor — create novel measurements.
17. Reality Compiler — restructure which actions exist.
18. Living Tool Network — temporary capability-sharing devices.
19. Invisible-Work Detector — find repetitive physical friction.
20. Affordance Scanner — identify alternative object functions.
21. Spatial Business Engine — AR-integrated real operations.
22. Programmable Environment — context-responsive devices.
23. Self-Assembling Microfactory — modular tools under constraints.
24. Business Seed Engine — launch small validated services.
25. Value Discovery Miner — find unused assets and unmet needs.
26. Living Grand Exchange — RPG interface to real commerce.
27. Self-Repairing Supply Chain — authorized alternative selection.
28. Opportunity Ecosystem — competing measured business pilots.
29. Verified-Outcome Economy — real evidence behind game progression.
30. Civilization Automation Fabric — permissioned federation among systems.

Novelty, engineering practicality, legality and social effects remain independent research questions.
