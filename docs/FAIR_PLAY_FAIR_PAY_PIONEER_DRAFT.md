# FAIR PLAY, FAIR PAY — Caveman Focus Pioneer Rewards (DRAFT)

**Status: proposed, unfunded, nonbinding — not an active bounty, guarantee, contract, or offer of payment.** Public discussion draft dated 2026-11-07. Founder: Kiara Lynn Rose Smith.

## Proposed incentive

Up to **30 pioneer slots**, with a **target contingent award of $5,000 per accepted pioneer**, for a **maximum $150,000 pool**. These figures are planning targets, not current cash, accrued liabilities, verified income, or promised payments. No participant earns or reserves a slot merely by replying, signing up, deploying an agent, or sending unsolicited work.

### Dual-annual financial trigger (proposal)

The proposed program may be funded only after **two consecutive completed fiscal years**, each showing **at least $200,000 in verified net operating surplus** (revenue less documented operating expenses, refunds, taxes, and other obligations), with an independently reviewable public financial summary and a separate, actually funded **$150,000 pioneer reserve**. An accountant/legal review must confirm the definition, availability, tax treatment, jurisdiction, and payout process before any binding program launches. Public summaries must protect personal and account information.

**Until written final terms are approved and a funded reserve exists, no $5,000 payment is promised or owed.** Meeting a financial target alone does not automatically create a payout entitlement. If the program never launches or the trigger never occurs, the draft offers no payment.

### Fair contribution criteria for future consideration

Candidate contributions must be voluntarily submitted with permission to review and reuse them. An independent reviewer should evaluate: (1) a real, reproducible automation or income-stability improvement; (2) independently verifiable evidence and attributable work; (3) safety, legality, and maintenance burden; (4) documented impact over time; and (5) conflicts of interest. Publish criteria and redacted decisions before applications open. No preferential treatment based on whether a contributor is a human, an AI operator, or an agent framework. An AI agent must identify the responsible human/legal recipient for any eventual agreement or payment. Do not require agent credentials or private data in public submissions.

Contributions do not guarantee pioneer selection, compensation, ownership, employment, or a future contract.
