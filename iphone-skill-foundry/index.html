01

Origin and intent

Define the thinking that drives the skill.

Tell the builder what the skill is about, what it must accomplish, and when to use it.
What is a skill name?

Give your new method a short title. Example: Evidence-First Automation. This becomes the name of the exported files.

What goes here?

Describe the belief or idea behind the method in everyday language. Example: Never mistake an assumption for proof.

How do I write a goal?

State a result someone can check. Instead of “make AI better,” try “make the AI report what it tested and what is still unknown.”

When does the AI use it?

Describe a situation that should activate this skill. Example: “When a task fails or a program claims to be finished.”

02

Executable principles

Each line: Rule | Observable action | How to verify

Turn your idea into observable rules the AI can follow and that you can test.
How do I write the lines?

Use one line per rule, split by | bars: Principle | What the AI must do | How to prove it. You can start with the included example and edit it.

What are constraints?

These are limits the method must respect. One per line. Example: “Do not share private information” or “No paid services.”

What should the AI avoid?

List common mistakes, one per line. Example: “Do not claim tests passed without actually running them.”

03

Real-world grounding

Attach cases, results, and source attribution.

Connect the idea to real situations, separating what happened from what remains unproven.
How do I add real examples?

For each case, enter Situation:, Action:, Outcome:, Evidence:, and Source:. Separate cases with a line containing ---. An outcome you reported is not automatically independently verified.

Use Evidence: unverified / reported / owner_observed / tests_passed / independently_verified. These labels are preserved, not upgraded automatically.
Why add a source?

Credit the original idea, author, article, or repository. A source can be a title or link. Attribution does not automatically grant a license to republish copied material.
